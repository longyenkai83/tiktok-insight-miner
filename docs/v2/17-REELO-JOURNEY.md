# 17 — Reelo Journey restoration

Owner's 2026-09-16 LONG-RUN DIRECTIVE is an accepted product/architecture correction.
Reelo is a personal-brand content journey system, not a random article generator.

Campaign goal → audience journey/awareness → sequence → source matching → one idea
→ content job/recipe → mode → Creative Plan → Writer/Critic/repair.

Customer Intelligence is **one legitimate source**, not the root of every Reelo piece.
This supersedes the universal interpretation of DEC-007. It does not change the Customer
Intelligence engine/schema or remove any human verification, angle selection, currentness,
evidence, Zone A/B or CIP requirement when an Insight source is actually used.

Story/experience-led, Insight-led, Knowledge/POV-led and Auto-discovery entry routes use
one Reelo writing engine. Exactly one primary and at most two useful supporting sources.
No forced Story/Insight/Knowledge, no fabricated Verified Insight for creator-led content.
Client Story keeps scoped permission/provenance; hypothetical future remains hypothetical.

Five awareness levels are a conceptual reference. Legacy cold/warm/hot/sell have contextual
ranges, not one-to-one equivalence; “hot” has pre-purchase and hot-sale meanings. CTA intent
comes from stage/job/goal; wording comes from creator voice. Early stages cannot accidentally
sell; authorized selling can clearly offer a real product. Stage labels are PROPOSED strategy,
not proof of audience beliefs, hidden motives, purchase readiness or demand.

GUIDED explores/calibrates a real sample then executes explicit remaining scope. AUTOPILOT
needs explicit campaign authorization and does not fabricate individual human approval.
Campaign-local calibration/history does not automatically rewrite creator voice or customer
truth. Final content/publish authority remains separate. No fixed ten-step sequence/ratios.

Implementation is opt-in in Reelo `integrations/journey/`; its README is the operational
contract and audit/crosswalk. Existing native host, purified writing knowledge and bounded
stage machine are reused. The operational J: vault and historical RP4 remain read-only.
Historical Markdown/JS routes remain legacy compatibility, not silently certified Journey.

RP4 article repair is PAUSED. After deterministic integration, restart diversified quality
benchmark, including a multi-piece offer journey. Current offer/campaign scope requires a
real owner decision; old creator campaign files do not supply new commercial authority.
No Notion, publish, main merge or Phase9 acceptance. Owner emotional quality review remains.
